### About me

I'm an AI master's student at Bocconi (on the Graduate Merit Award), coming from a BSc in Economics, Management and Computer Science (110 cum laude) with an exchange semester at the National University of Singapore.

Honestly, I'm curious about pretty much everything in AI, but a few things keep pulling me back:

- 🎲 **Game theory & multi-agent RL:** what it means to play well when everyone else is learning too. My thesis looked at how to spot and exploit a risk-averse poker opponent, which turned out to be a great excuse to play a lot of poker "for research".
- 📐 **Optimization & inference:** the maths underneath learning. Why algorithms converge, how to reason under uncertainty, and how to make it all actually run.
- 🧠 **Deep learning internals:** I like to understand things by rebuilding them, which is why I'm writing a GPT in plain NumPy, gradients and all.

**Currently working on:**
- 🔧 Finishing the layers and training pipeline for [my_gpt](https://github.com/costadavide/my_gpt)
- 🃏 Teaching an RL agent to play [Macchiavelli](https://github.com/costadavide/macchiavelli_rl), the card game I grew up losing at

**Along the way:**
- 🎓 MSc in AI at Bocconi: optimization & inference algorithms, mathematical methods in CS, software engineering
- 📈 **Co-head of AlgoTrade**, Bocconi Student Quantitative Finance, leading Bocconi's first official team in algorithmic trading competitions
- 🏛️ **Co-founder** of Bocconi's first quantitative finance association (80+ active members)
- 🥉 **3rd of 20 teams** in the Deutsche Bank Asset Management Competition (+11.2% in 6 weeks, Monte Carlo portfolio optimization in Python)
- 🃏 Off-screen: sudoku, poker, Macchiavelli, hiking, and 17 countries visited so far

### Featured projects

| Project | What it is | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| [**thesis_project**](https://github.com/costadavide/thesis_project) | **Exploiting Cumulative Prospect Theory-based CFR agents in Leduc Poker.** Builds a risk-averse opponent by reweighting Counterfactual Regret Minimization with a CPT value and probability-weighting function, then exploits it with Explore-Then-Commit agents (suit-isomorphism abstraction, confidence thresholds) and benchmarks them against Nash play. | Python, OpenSpiel, NumPy |
| [**my_gpt**](https://github.com/costadavide/my_gpt) | **A decoder-only Transformer written from scratch in NumPy.** Forward and backward passes for every layer (softmax, linear, LayerNorm, attention, embeddings, feed-forward) with vectorized, numerically stable gradients and derivations documented in the README. *Work in progress.* | Python, NumPy |
| [**macchiavelli_rl**](https://github.com/costadavide/macchiavelli_rl) | Reinforcement learning for **Macchiavelli**, the Italian rummy-style card game. *Work in progress.* | Python |
| [**Machine_learning_project**](https://github.com/costadavide/Machine_learning_project) | End-to-end tabular prediction challenge: feature engineering, random forest and XGBoost iterations, hyperparameter tuning. | Python, XGBoost, scikit-learn |
| [**assignment_sno**](https://github.com/costadavide/assignment_sno) | Social network analysis of an organization from communication, event-attendance and demographic data. | Python, pandas |
